Privacy Policy

Sand Alchemy

Effective as of 2026-09-03

This privacy policy applies to the Sand Alchemy app (hereby referred to as the "Application") for mobile devices, created by Melodeon (hereby referred to as the "Service Provider") as a free service. The Application is a physics-based sand puzzle game in which falling sand particles settle, combine and transform along alchemy chains. It is provided for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed to play offline: the game simulation runs on your device and your progress is stored there. It does, however, use integrated third-party services for advertising, analytics and stability reporting, which collect certain information.

Through those third-party SDKs, information including your Android Advertising ID, device identifiers and general device and app information (such as device model, operating system version and app version) may be collected.

The Application uses Firebase Analytics to collect aggregated usage and event data, and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash and error reports. This data is processed by Google and is not used by the Service Provider to identify you personally. The Firebase SDK is started with privacy-safe defaults, meaning data collection stays disabled until the consent described below has been obtained.

Advertising is served through Google AdMob, which may collect device and advertising identifiers in order to select and measure ads.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

The Application uses the third-party services listed below, which may collect information used to identify you. Links to their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config)

Third Party Access

The Service Provider shares data with third parties only through the official SDKs listed above, which are used to deliver advertisements, measure app usage and report crashes. No other tracking tools or data-sharing arrangements are implemented, and the Service Provider does not sell your data.

The Application does not integrate Google Play Games Services, offers no account linking, and does not synchronise your progress to the cloud.

Consent and Ad Personalization

Where required by law — in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — the Application shows a consent message on first launch using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any personalized advertising data is collected. You choose there whether advertising partners may use your data for personalized ads.

You can reopen that choice at any time from the Application's settings screen, where a privacy options entry is shown to users in the regions the consent form applies to. Outside those regions the entry is hidden because no consent form applies.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all use of the Application at any time by uninstalling it, using the standard uninstall process available on your device or through the app marketplace. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data described above.

You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier at any time from your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads), independently of the in-app consent choice above.

Data Retention and Deletion

The Service Provider does not operate its own servers and does not collect, store or transmit your data to any infrastructure under its own control. Your game progress and settings remain on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the Application.

Information processed by the third-party services listed above is retained and deleted according to their own privacy policies. Google's data controls, including deletion of advertising and analytics data associated with your identifiers, are available through your Google account settings.

Children

The Application is a sand puzzle game suitable for a general audience. Because it displays advertising, its target audience is set to users aged 13 and older. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and it will be promptly addressed.

Security

The Service Provider holds no personal data on its own infrastructure, so there is no such data at risk there. Locally stored progress remains under your own device's security controls. Data handled by the third-party services above is protected under their respective security practices.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The Service Provider will notify users of any changes by updating this page, and the effective date at the top will change accordingly. Continued use of the Application after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Your Consent

By using the Application, you consent to this Privacy Policy as amended from time to time.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, or wish to make a request regarding your data, contact the developer directly through the official Google Play Store developer contact page for Melodeon.

Sand Alchemy is developed by Melodeon. This policy covers the Android application only.
