Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Barcode Battle app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, created by Melodeon (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed to run primarily offline. However, it may collect and use certain information through integrated third-party services for advertising, player authentication, and operational purposes.

For a better experience, while using our Application, the Service Provider may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information (via third-party SDKs), including but not limited to Android Advertising ID, device identifiers, and gaming profile identifiers.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

Account Linking and Progress Recovery (Google Play Games Services)

The Application integrates Google Play Games Services to support automatic sign-in, account linking, and cross-device game progress recovery. To enable these features seamlessly, the Application utilizes Google's Recall API. This API may share limited end-user device and identity data with Google to look up or establish an account link, even if the user does not have a fully configured or active public Google Play Games Services profile. Users can manage or restrict this data sharing at any time via the official Google Play Games application settings on their device.

The Application does use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Here are the links to the privacy policies of the third-party service providers used by the Application:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase

Third Party Access

The Service Provider shares data with third parties exclusively through integrated official SDKs necessary to deliver advertisements and measure app usage. The Application integrates the Google AdMob SDK for advertising purposes and Google Analytics for Firebase for usage analytics and app performance measurement. No other unauthorized tracking tools or data-sharing operations are implemented.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all use of the Application at any time by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall process available on your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace. You can also disconnect or manage your Google account linking via your device's Google Play Games app settings menu.

Data Retention Policy

The Application does not collect, store, or transmit any user data on its own private servers. Game saves and puzzle progress created within the Application remain on the user's device, or may be securely synced to Google's cloud infrastructure via Google Play Games Services if you choose to utilize cloud features. Information processed by third-party SDKs (like AdMob and Google Play Services) is handled according to their respective privacy policies.

Children

The Application is a logic puzzle game suitable for all audiences. To comply with advertising policies, the target audience is restricted to users aged 13 and older. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and it will be promptly addressed.

Security

Since the Application collects no personal data directly on developer-owned servers, there is no personal information at risk on our infrastructure. All local game saves remain under the user's own device security controls, and cloud data is secured by Google's infrastructure.
